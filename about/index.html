About Dixieland Tree Services

For more than 30 years, Dixieland Tree Services has helped property owners across North Alabama handle tree removal projects safely and efficiently. We specialize in large, tall, hazardous, and difficult-to-access trees, including jobs that require more than standard tree removal equipment.

From towering pines and massive oaks to trees positioned over homes or inside tight spaces, our team has the experience and equipment needed to approach challenging removals with care. Our goal is simple: complete the work safely, protect the surrounding property, and leave the jobsite clean when the work is finished.

What We Do

Built for Difficult Tree Removal

Not every tree removal is straightforward. Large trees, confined backyards, storm-damaged trees, and trees located close to homes or other structures can require specialized equipment and a carefully planned approach.

Dixieland Tree Services is equipped to handle these demanding projects. Our remote-controlled equipment allows our team to work in locations where conventional tree removal equipment may be difficult to use. This capability helps us take on large and complicated removals throughout Huntsville, Madison, and surrounding North Alabama communities.

Our tree services include:

  • Large and tall tree removal
  • Hazardous tree removal
  • Emergency tree removal
  • Storm damage cleanup
  • Difficult and tight-access tree removal
  • Stump grinding
  • Complete jobsite cleanup
Experience

More Than 30 Years of Experience

Experience matters when large trees are located near homes, buildings, fences, driveways, and other valuable property.

With more than 30 years of experience, Dixieland Tree Services understands the planning and attention required for challenging tree removal projects. Before work begins, the property and tree can be evaluated so the appropriate removal approach can be determined.

That experience is especially valuable when dealing with trees that are unusually large, unstable, storm damaged, or positioned in locations that make access difficult.

Safety First

Safety and Property Protection Come First

Tree removal involves more than getting a tree on the ground. The surrounding home, landscaping, structures, and property also need to be considered throughout the process.

Dixieland Tree Services uses professional equipment and proven removal methods designed for demanding tree projects. We are fully insured and carry workers' compensation coverage, giving customers additional confidence when hiring a tree service for work on their property.

Our credentials include:

  • BBB A+ Accredited
  • Fully insured
  • Workers' compensation coverage
  • Google Guaranteed
  • 30+ years of tree service experience
Our Equipment

Specialized Equipment for Large Trees and Tight Spaces

Some of the most difficult tree removal projects happen where space is limited.

Trees growing behind homes, close to structures, or in areas with restricted equipment access require a different approach than trees standing in an open field. Our specialized remote-controlled equipment gives us additional options for handling these situations while helping reduce unnecessary impact on the surrounding yard.

This capability is one of the reasons Dixieland Tree Services can take on large and difficult removals that require specialized planning and equipment.

24/7 Response

Ready When Storms and Tree Emergencies Happen

Tree problems do not always happen during normal business hours. High winds, severe storms, damaged limbs, and unstable trees can create immediate concerns for homes and properties.

Dixieland Tree Services provides 24/7 emergency response for urgent tree removal and storm damage situations. When a dangerous tree needs attention quickly, our team can evaluate the situation and determine the safest approach for removing the hazard.

Our Process

A Straightforward Approach From Estimate to Cleanup

We believe customers should understand what to expect before tree removal begins.

The process starts with a request for an estimate followed by an on-site assessment. Once the tree and surrounding property have been evaluated, the removal can be planned around the specific conditions of the property.

After the tree has been safely removed, our team handles cleanup of the work area. Stump grinding can also be included when needed.
